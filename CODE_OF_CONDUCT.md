# Code of conduct

We want Salt TUI discussions to be useful and respectful for everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and contributors with different experience levels.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept corrections graciously.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not publish private messages, credentials, or identifying system data without permission.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed for Salt TUI. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to uphold it. They will consider context and aim for a proportionate response.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when investigation or action requires sharing details.
